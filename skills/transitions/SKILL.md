# Transitions

Choose transitions that express a relationship between scenes: match cut, shared-object movement, shape morph, directional wipe, camera move, or restrained dissolve. Maintain spatial or conceptual continuity where useful. Avoid using one generic transition repeatedly. Specify outgoing and incoming states, overlap, duration, direction, and any audio cue. Never let a transition obscure essential narration or create a blank/unchanged interval.

**Deliver:** transition type, outgoing/incoming states, timing, direction, and continuity notes.

**Quality checks:** transitions connect ideas, preserve readability, and do not repeat mechanically.
