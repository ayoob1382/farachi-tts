# Visual Effects

Use masks, particles, glow, blur, shadows, trails, distortion, and depth sparingly to reinforce hierarchy or physical behavior. Effects must remain performant in browser-based rendering and degrade gracefully. Avoid excessive glow, noisy particles, gratuitous camera shake, and effects that reduce text contrast. Specify effect parameters, lifetime, layering, and cleanup so effects do not persist accidentally into later scenes.

**Deliver:** effect purpose, parameters, layering, lifetime, and performance notes.

**Quality checks:** effects support the message, remain legible, and do not leave visual artifacts.
